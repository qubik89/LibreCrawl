## Design Context

### Users
SEO professionals use LibreCrawl to inspect a technical crawl, understand the scale of issues, and move directly into the affected URLs or incidents.

### Brand Personality
Sobrio, preciso, fiable. The interface should feel evidence-led and calm, with emphasis reserved for materially important risk.

### Aesthetic Direction
Refined dark operational workspace. Use charcoal surfaces, compact typography, and semantic green, amber, red, and blue accents. Avoid decorative gradients, excessive purple, and dashboard-card clutter.

### Design Principles
- Show the denominator and the affected scope for conclusions.
- Prefer direct drilldowns over explanatory UI copy.
- Keep monitoring states clear without inventing unavailable data.
- Make dense technical information scannable and keyboard accessible.
